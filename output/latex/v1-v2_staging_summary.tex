Source scope &
\texttt{index.md} was parsed as a requirement, creating one non-requirement record and index-specific fields. &
Exclude \texttt{index.md} before requirement parsing. \\

Identity/provenance redundancy &
The fields \texttt{filename}, \texttt{slug}, \texttt{title}, and \texttt{id} all contain the same identifying information, while none contains only the requirement ID. &
Extract the tagged requirement ID into a single identity field and retain one separate field for provenance from the Algoritmekader. \\

Website metadata &
\texttt{hide}, \texttt{allow\_excel\_export}, and \texttt{summary} represented website configuration or presentation rather than requirement content. &
Remove these fields from the cleaned requirement dataset. \\

Over-parsed Markdown headings &
The \texttt{\#\#} headings in \texttt{aia-38} and \texttt{avg-10} were incorrectly converted into separate one-off fields, although they belonged to the explanatory text. &
Merge the affected subsections into \texttt{section\_toelichting}. \\

Serialized multi-valued fields &
\texttt{levenscyclus}, \texttt{onderwerp}, \texttt{soort-toepassing}, \texttt{risicogroep}, \texttt{rol-ai-act}, \texttt{transparantieverplichting}, \texttt{systeemrisico}, and \texttt{rollen} were analysed as serialized list combinations rather than individual values. List order also created artificial distinctions. &
Preserve lists during parsing and expand them into individual requirement--field--value assignments before analysis. \\

Rudimentary semantic classification &
Cardinality-based rules misclassified fields, e.g.\ \texttt{levenscyclus} as free text and \texttt{uitzondering} as Boolean metadata. &
Replace heuristic classification with explicit semantic field types based on source inspection. \\

Generated relationship placeholders &
\texttt{section\_maatregelen} and \texttt{section\_hulpmiddelen} contained generation directives rather than substantive prose. &
Treat these as references to separate entities and import the corresponding source files in later iterations. \\
